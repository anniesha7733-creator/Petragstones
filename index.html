Our Approach

FROM ORDER TO SHIPMENT

Every order is handled with care, precision and attention to detail — from the moment we receive your requirements until the material is prepared and dispatched.

Open Video in Full Screen / Separate Page
01

Understand Your Requirements

We begin by understanding the exact requirements of the order — whether you need blocks, slabs, tiles or cut-to-size material. Quantity, dimensions, thickness, finish, grade, packing requirements and delivery destination are carefully reviewed before production begins.

02

Select & Process

Once the order is confirmed, the appropriate material is selected and processed according to the agreed specifications. Depending on the requirement, the stone may be prepared as a raw block, finished slab, tile or customised cut-to-size piece.

03

Quality Inspection

Before packing, every order undergoes a detailed quality inspection by our team. We personally inspect the material for:

  • Colour and natural variation
  • Veining and overall appearance
  • Dimensions and thickness
  • Surface finish
  • Visible cracks, chips or defects
  • Quantity and order specifications
We do not dispatch material until it has been inspected and approved by our team.

04

Careful Packing & Preparation

After final inspection, the material is carefully prepared for transportation. Packing and loading are carried out with the nature, size and format of the material in mind to help ensure safe handling during transit.

05

Final Verification Before Dispatch

Before the shipment leaves our facility, we conduct a final verification of the material, quantity, packing and shipping documentation. Our objective is simple: what leaves our facility should match what was approved for the order.

06

Dispatch & Shipment

Once everything has been checked and approved, the shipment is dispatched to the buyer's designated destination through the agreed logistics arrangement. From blocks and slabs to tiles and customised pieces, we maintain the same attention to quality throughout the process.

Manufacturing & Production

Marble Manufacturing Process: From Block to Tile

01. Quarrying & Block Extraction

Premium-quality marble is extracted from natural quarries using advanced cutting techniques. Large marble blocks are carefully selected based on their color, veining, and structural quality.

02. Block Cutting

The selected marble blocks are transported to the processing facility, where advanced gang saws or block-cutting machines cut them into slabs of the required thickness.

03. Slab Processing & Polishing

The slabs undergo surface calibration, resin treatment when required, and progressive polishing to enhance their natural patterns, smoothness, and finish.

04. Tile Cutting & Sizing

Processed marble slabs are cut into tiles of specified dimensions using precision cutting machines. Edges and thickness are checked to meet the required project specifications.

05. Quality Inspection

Each tile is inspected for surface finish, dimensions, edge quality, and visible defects. Tiles are sorted according to their shade, pattern, and quality grade.

06. Packaging & Export

The finished tiles are carefully packed in protective materials and sturdy wooden crates, ready for secure transportation and international delivery.
